Ultrasound Imaging & Acoustic Structured Illumination

University of Oklahoma · Graduate Research

Developed multi-beam Acoustic Structured Illumination patterns using Gerchberg–Saxton and STAR phase-retrieval algorithms. Evaluated beamforming and imaging performance through simulation and hardware experiments.

Implemented Golay, Barker, and Hadamard coded-excitation approaches and integrated multi-angle plane-wave and super-resolution ultrasound workflows.

Developed a hydrophone-based laboratory setup for measuring and characterizing ultrasound pressure fields and validating acoustic beam profiles.
